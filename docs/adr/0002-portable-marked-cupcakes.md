# Make Cupcakes portable marked portfolio items

Cake needs to represent standalone outcomes that consume portfolio capacity without inventing a parent Cake. A Cupcake is therefore the same Trello card moving whole among Pantry, Cake Stand, and Plate, with a `🧁` title prefix as its visible type marker and a parentless Outcome/Success contract; Cake may apply that marker after classification and explicit approval. We chose the title marker over a label or hidden type field because the distinction must remain obvious in Cake's board interface, while the moving card preserves its user-managed Task checklist. If follow-on outcomes emerge, the Cupcake is promoted into a Cake whose current outcome becomes its first Slice.
